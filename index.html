About the club

The 1890 Club was founded in 2000 to build stronger links between former players and the current hockey clubs at Oxford and Cambridge. It supports the social side of the university clubs and gives members a way to stay in touch.

Members are invited to hockey matches and social events with old team-mates, and former opposition. They also hear news of the current university teams. Their subscriptions provide valuable support for OUHC and CUHC, particularly around the Varsity Match.

What we do

Hockey

  • 1890 Oxford v Cambridge matches on Varsity day
  • Men’s, women’s and mixed alumni fixtures
  • Games against the current Blues and invited sides

Social

  • Pre-Varsity drinks
  • The 1890 Dinner, held every five years
  • Regular newsletters for members

Supporting the clubs

  • Helping OUHC and CUHC stage the Varsity Match
  • Post-Varsity celebrations for current players
